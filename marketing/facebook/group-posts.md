Posted AS the TellMore AI Page (owner's choice, 2026-09-27). Every post and every
comment is approved by the owner before it goes out. Rules below were read from
each group's About page on 2026-09-27. Facts used are only those listed in
`posts.md` (3-day trial, no card; Shop from ৳2,699/month; Service from ৳2,299/month;
Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp, website; Bangla, English, Banglish).

is flagged by Facebook as spam — reach drops, posting gets blocked, and the Page
(which also runs TellMore's own bot) can be restricted. So: few groups, a
different post for each, at most 2–3 groups a day.

## D — AI Automation Bangladesh · builder story (with the English reel)

We built an AI chatbot for Bangladeshi businesses — here's what was hard. 🤖🇧🇩

TellMore AI answers customers on Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp and a website widget, from the business's own catalogue and documents. Three things took far more work than expected:

1️⃣ Banglish. Customers write "dam koto", "ase?", "xl size hbe?" — mixed scripts, no fixed spelling. The bot has to answer in whatever the customer wrote in.
2️⃣ Photos and voice notes. Many customers never type the product name — they send a screenshot or a voice message. So the bot recognises products from a photo and understands voice notes.
3️⃣ One-click setup. A business owner should never have to find a Page ID or paste a token. Log in with Facebook, pick the Page, done.

It also takes orders in the chat and books meetings into Google Calendar for service businesses.

Would love feedback from this group — especially from anyone who has built Bangla chat flows. What broke for you?

3-day free trial if you want to try it: tellmoreai.com
