ABOUT TELLMORE AI
TellMore AI (website: https://www.tellmoreai.com) is an AI customer-service chatbot for businesses in Bangladesh. It answers customers automatically, 24 hours a day, on Facebook Messenger, Instagram DM, WhatsApp and a chat widget on the business's own website. It replies in Bangla and English, in whichever language the customer writes.

WHO IT IS FOR
Online shops that sell through Facebook pages and Instagram (clothing, cosmetics, electronics, food and so on), and service businesses (agencies, clinics, coaching centres, consultants, real estate, travel).

WHAT IT DOES
- Answers customer questions about products, prices, delivery, payment and policies from the business's own information.
- Shops: matches a product photo a customer sends to the right product in the catalogue, shows product photos, takes orders and records them in the dashboard.
- Services: answers from the business's own documents (PDF, Word, text) and books meetings straight into Google Calendar with a Google Meet link.
- Understands voice messages.
- Replies to comments on Facebook and Instagram posts, publicly and in private message.
- Broadcasts and follow-up messages to recent customers, only inside Meta's 24-hour messaging window.
- Hands a conversation to a human when needed; the owner can reply by hand from the same inbox. The owner's own replies are never counted against the package.
- AI Assistant inside the dashboard: helps the owner add products, set offers and answer questions about the business.
- Analytics: messages, orders or bookings, and how the bot is doing.

SETUP
- No code and no technical knowledge needed. Facebook Page, Instagram and WhatsApp connect with one click; the business never copies an ID or a token.
- Sign up at https://www.tellmoreai.com, fill in the business profile, teach the bot about the business (a few sentences is enough), connect a channel. Most businesses are live the same day.
- Full manual: https://www.tellmoreai.com/docs

RUNNING LOW IN THE MIDDLE OF THE MONTH
When anything reaches 90% (bot replies, products or documents, AI Assistant questions) the dashboard warns the owner and offers "Add more". Raising a number in the middle of the month costs only the days left; it applies as soon as the payment is approved and from the next renewal it is part of the package. Nothing is deleted when a limit is reached; if bot replies run out, the bot pauses until more are added or the month turns, and the owner can still reply by hand.

HOW TO BUY
1. Choose a package on https://www.tellmoreai.com/pricing (or in the dashboard's Billing tab), our AI or own AI key, monthly or yearly, and raise any numbers if needed.
2. Create the account (or sign in).
3. Pay by bKash or Nagad as Send Money to the number shown in Billing, then enter the transaction ID and the number paid from. Online payment by card or mobile banking is offered on the payment step when it is available.
4. The payment is checked, usually within a few hours. The owner is told on the phone and by email the moment it is approved, and the dashboard opens by itself. One transaction ID can be used for one purchase only.
While a first payment is being checked, the account can use the free trial if it has not been used before.
If a payment is not approved, the reason is shown in Billing with a "Try again" button.
Renewing: the dashboard's Billing tab shows the customer's own package with Renew, Update package and Add more.
